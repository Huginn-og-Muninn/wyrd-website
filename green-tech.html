Green tech and non-profits

Green tech is the work we like best.

It's also where most of our experience is. Bo co-built the software that ran three of the top-performing large grid batteries in Texas: systems with thousands of moving parts that have to run around the clock. We bring that experience to solar, storage and other clean energy companies, from forecasting and optimization to production systems built to stay up.

Non-profits and science

Mission-driven organizations often run on small teams and a lot of data. Take a non-profit doing fisheries science: we can automate the data acquisition, from field surveys and sensor feeds to public river and ocean datasets, and run their population models on a schedule, deployed and monitored. The scientists spend their time on the science instead of the pipeline, and every report starts from data that is already clean and current.

Non-profits and social-justice organizations get reduced rates. Tell us about your mission when you get in touch.

Responsible with energy and security

AI has shifted energy and security priorities, and we build with both in mind. We use smaller models wherever they do the job, which cuts energy use and carbon, and we put guardrails on our agents to keep out slop and, more importantly, security risks.
